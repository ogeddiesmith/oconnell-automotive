What I do

Car repair in Kankakee and the towns around it.

If you are not sure what is wrong, just tell me what it is doing. A video of the noise tells me more than anything you could write down.

Brake repair

Pads, rotors, calipers, hoses and lines. Grinding, squealing, a soft pedal or a shudder when you stop. Most common job there is and the one people ride out too long.

Oil changes and fluids

Oil and filter, transmission fluid, coolant, differentials, power steering. Cheap to do on time. Expensive not to.

Batteries, alternators and starters

Clicks and will not turn over, dash lights flickering, dead every morning. I test the whole charging system instead of just selling you a battery.

Radiators and cooling

Radiators, water pumps, thermostats, hoses, heater cores. If the temp gauge is climbing, stop driving it and call me. Overheating turns a small bill into an engine.

Check engine light and diagnostics

I scan it and then tell you what it actually means. A code points at a system, not a part, and plenty of places will sell you the part.

Suspension and steering

Struts, shocks, control arms, ball joints, tie rods, sway bar links, wheel bearings. Clunking over bumps, wandering on the highway, a hum that changes when you turn.

Belts, hoses and leaks

Serpentine belts, tensioners, idlers, pulleys. Squeal on a cold start, or a puddle under the car you want identified before it gets worse.

Tune ups and ignition

Spark plugs, coils, wires, filters. Misfire, rough idle, no power on a hill, gas mileage falling off a cliff.

Sensors and electrical

Oxygen sensors, mass airflow, crank and cam sensors, wiring faults. The stuff that makes a car run wrong without anything looking broken.

Pre purchase inspections

Buying something off Marketplace? Bring it by before you hand over the money. Cheapest hour you will ever spend.

Why one mechanic

Same guy every time.

Big shops move you through whoever is open and hand you to a service writer who has never seen your car. Here it is one person. The guy who figured out what was wrong is the guy who fixed it and the guy who hands you the keys.

  • You talk to the mechanic. Not a counter, not a call center. Question about the repair? You are already talking to whoever did it.
  • I learn your car. Second time out, I already know what I put on it and what I told you to watch.
  • No upsell sheet. I will tell you what it needs now, what can wait and what you can leave alone. A lot can wait. Somebody should tell you that.
  • You see the old parts. Every job. If I say the rotors were done, they will be sitting there.
  • Word of mouth is the whole business. Most of my work comes from somebody sending somebody. That only holds up if I do right by the person in front of me.
